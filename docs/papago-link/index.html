1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Shenzhen Haotek Technology Co., Limited (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the mobile application PAPAGO!Link (“the App”). PAPAGO!Link is a dash camera (dashcam) companion application.

By installing or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information transparently and securely.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in three categories:

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Information you choose to enter or provide while using the App, such as configuration settings and preferences.
  • Any content you actively create, save, or share through the App’s features.

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, application version, and basic device identifiers used to provide and improve the service.
  • Usage information: anonymous diagnostic and crash information that helps us fix bugs and improve reliability.

2.3 Information Collected from Permissions

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, the App may access the following data. We only access this data to provide the specific feature you request, and we never collect it without your permission:

  • Video / camera content — recorded when you use the recording feature. Recorded media is stored locally on your device by default and is not automatically uploaded to our servers.
  • Audio content (optional) — collected only if you explicitly enable audio recording.
  • Location data (optional) — collected only if you enable GPS tagging, and can be turned off at any time.
  • Stored files — saved videos and settings you choose to keep on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the App and its features;
  • Provide customer support and respond to your requests;
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve reliability and performance;
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our legal rights.

We do not sell your personal information. Unless you explicitly choose to share or sync content, your locally generated data (for example, recorded media) remains on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

4. Device Permissions We Request

To provide the features of the App, we may request the following permissions. You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings. Revoking a permission may limit certain features but will not prevent you from using the rest of the App.

  • Camera — to record driving video through your connected dash camera.
  • Microphone (optional) — to record in-cabin or event audio, only if you enable it.
  • Location (optional) — to attach GPS-based location tags to recordings, only if you enable it.
  • Storage — to save recorded videos to your device’s local storage.
  • Background / ongoing operation (optional) — to continue recording in the background if you turn on that feature.

5. Sharing of Information and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With service providers that help us operate the App (for example, hosting or analytics), who are bound by confidentiality obligations;
  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process;
  • To protect our rights, safety, and the safety of others.

Third-party services accessed through the App are governed by their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

6. Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. Content you store locally on your device remains under your control and can be deleted by you at any time.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correction: request that we correct inaccurate information;
  • Deletion: request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal requirements;
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw any consent you previously gave, including by revoking device permissions or uninstalling the App;
  • Object or restrict: object to or request restriction of certain processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 10. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can delete it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page and update the “Last updated” date at the top. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
